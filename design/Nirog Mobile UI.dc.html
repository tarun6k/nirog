NIROG निरोग — field diagnosis UI, first canvas

15 artboards at 360×800dp. Industry's blueprint grammar (square corners, hairline frames, registration marks, condensed Latin headings) recolored to the brief's field palette: deep green #1d5b38 action · ochre #8a5305 caution/chemical · clay #a03024 alert, on warm paper neutrals. Hindi (Noto Sans Devanagari) leads every screen; English rides along in Barlow Condensed. Body ≥16sp, key numbers ≥24sp tabular, targets ≥56dp, every color paired with an icon + label, a mic on every screen. Dashed frames are photo drop-slots — drop real leaf photos in and they persist.
